Recivvy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Applies to the Recivvy app for iPhone.

In short: Recivvy does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Recivvy stores

Recivvy stores the following on your device only:

  • The names of the people and groups you add.
  • Your expenses, their items, and who shared them.
  • The receipt photos and PDFs you scan or upload.
  • Your settings, such as light or dark appearance.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Recivvy has no accounts, no sign-in and no server, and the app makes no network requests.

Camera, photos and files

Recivvy asks for the camera only when you choose to scan a receipt, and reads a photo or file only when you pick one. The text on the receipt is recognised on your device. The image is kept with the expense on your device and is not uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. Recivvy contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We cannot see your groups, expenses, receipts or how you use the app.

Sharing

Recivvy shares nothing by itself. When you choose "Share as PDF", your device's share sheet opens and you decide where the PDF goes. That PDF contains the expense, each person's share, and the receipt image.

Other people's information

The names you type for other people stay on your device like everything else. Please only share a PDF with people who should see it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete any expense, group or person inside the app. Deleting the app deletes all of its data. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, we cannot restore it.

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Recivvy's data. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

Children

Recivvy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by adding an optional online backup, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
