Where is my data kept?

Only on your iPhone. Recivvy has no account and no server. Receipts are read on the phone itself.

Can I get my data back after deleting the app?

No. Deleting Recivvy deletes its groups, expenses and receipt photos, and there is no copy anywhere else.

The scan missed an item or got a price wrong

Every field on the receipt screen can be edited. Tap a name or price to change it, use "Add item" for anything missing, and the bin icon to remove a line. Recivvy warns you when the items, tax and tip do not add up to the receipt total.

How does tax work?

Tick "Taxable" on the items that were taxed. Then type the tax amount printed on the receipt, or type the rate and Recivvy works out the amount. Tax is shared only across the ticked items.

What is the difference between a group and one person?

A group has its own balance between its members. An expense with one person goes on a separate balance between just the two of you. The two are never added together.

How do other people see the split?

Open a saved expense and choose "Share as PDF". The PDF shows each person's share, the items, and the original receipt. They do not need the app.

Which receipts work best?

Recivvy is built for English-language receipts from the United States and Canada, with amounts in dollars.
